# Zemy privacy policy

_Last updated: 8 November 2026_

Zemy does not collect, store or share any personal data.

- **No data leaves your Mac.** Images, SVGs, colors, file names and clipboard history are processed on your Mac with Apple's on-device frameworks. Zemy makes no network requests.
- **No accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking.** Zemy has no servers and contains no third-party SDKs.
- **Clipboard history** is kept only on your Mac, inside Zemy's own storage. It skips items marked as passwords by password managers. You can turn it off, clear it, or stop it being kept after you quit, from the Clipboard tool.
- **Files** are read only when you open, drop or paste them, and written only where you choose to save or export.

If you have questions, open an issue at https://github.com/sudheer-salavadi/zemy/issues.
